Mathematical novelty ranking

1. Extend the n105 selected-separator dual to a quantified orientation box.
   Evidence: the existing exact 103-term nonnegative dual already certifies
   the fixed-orientation problem to within 1.498e-45, while allowing every
   center to move. This is an actual proof object, not a numerical Hessian
   claim. All 23,520 constraints and a working rational verifier are available.

   Proposed theorem scope: fix the recorded owner/axis/sign for every pair,
   but allow each rational half-angle parameter t_i to vary independently
   in [t_i^0-epsilon,t_i^0+epsilon]. Allow all centers and L to vary under
   containment. Reuse the fixed nonnegative weights. The weighted inequality
   is c(t).X+c_L L+b(t)>=0. Enclose b(t)<=b_plus and
   K(t)=c_L+sum_j |c_j(t)|/2 <= K_plus using outward rational intervals.
   If K_plus>0 and b_plus<0, every such feasible packing satisfies
   L >= -b_plus/K_plus. State epsilon and the exact lower rational. The t-box
   is not an angle box in radians; no separator switch or global optimum is
   covered. No interval certificate or quantitative new bound is claimed yet.

   One small diagnostic experiment, proposed only: implement interval
   evaluation for the 103 weighted rows at epsilon=2^-40, 2^-60 and 2^-80.
   Reconstruct row numbering from all choices, preserve cancellations at the
   base point, and compare the epsilon=0 result exactly to the existing dual.
   Check whether a useful lower bound survives a nonzero epsilon; archive the
   exact coefficients and a verifier if it does. No optimization is required.

   Likely cost: a few hours to implement/review interval bounds; a hard
   10-minute total computational diagnostic cap, single process. These are
   estimates, not measured costs. Early stop on epsilon=0 mismatch, invalid
   denominator enclosure, loss of the required signs, or exceeding the cap.
   If all three boxes give U-minus-lower >= 1e-12, record that this simple
   extension is too weak at those widths and stop; do not shrink indefinitely
   just to manufacture a theorem. Even success would be a modest new scoped
   robustness theorem, not unrestricted local optimality.
