Mathematics, methods, implementation lineage and review scope

Let s(n) be the least square side accommodating n freely rotated unit squares
with disjoint interiors. Each witness row gives rational x,y,t. Put
  c=(1-t*t)/(1+t*t), d=2*t/(1+t*t).
Its vertices are (x,y)+(a(c,d)+b(-d,c))/2, a,b in {-1,1}, inside [-U/2,U/2]^2.
The identity c*c+d*d=1 is exact. Separating projections on one of the two
edge normals of either square prove pairwise nonoverlap by the separating-axis
theorem. Touching is allowed. No decimal approximation, floating result, solver
convergence, numerical rank, or tolerance enters the finite-witness proof.

The retained first checker (code/independent_verify.py) and second checker
(code/published_verify.py) reconstruct cyclic polygons independently and
explicitly check unit edges and orthogonality. The additional checker
(code/legacy_exact_verify.py) uses rational center/basis projections. Its
directions are unit by construction; verify.py supplies external count,
schema, rational-string and positive-side checks. The legacy file includes
a float mode, but this package invokes verify_exact directly and never that
mode. The first checker also has numerical screening routines, which are
not invoked. The second and dual checker use assertions: the driver refuses
Python optimization before loading them.

All four retained checker files are copied byte for byte from the project's
src/. The two geometry checkers and legacy implementation were reused from
the preceding n68/frontier work; sources/historical/reused_code.json records
that lineage. The restricted dual checker is the preceding frontier campaign's
separate implementation. The records do not identify separate human authors
for these campaign files. They are agent-assisted project code, not claimed
to be authored by Couzo, Evan Daniel, or three outside reviewers. This release
adds the inventory/hash/comparison/control driver and portable documentation,
prepared locally by Codex. No retained geometry logic was edited.

Implementation independence has limited scope: the geometry checkers share
the witness schema, half-angle identity, separating-axis theorem, Python
interpreter and fractions.Fraction arithmetic. They import no optimizer,
repair code or shared project geometry helper. They are not independent
arithmetic hardware and are not a proof-assistant development. The third
implementation is the existing legacy code, not the unavailable checker from
the external ChatGPT review. Its pair count in reports is derived from its
full loops, whereas the first two checkers expose enumeration counters.

Discovery used independent angle variables, selected contact equations and
coordinate gauges, high-precision Newton polishing, then rational half-angle
export and exact outward repair. n130 closed the previously slack (21,28)
contact and followed that branch. n292 resolved a nearly axial corner square
after broad snapping failed. These are nearby refinements: the retained pose
comparison found no robust separator-owner change. A new contact incidence
alone does not demonstrate a new combinatorial arrangement.
