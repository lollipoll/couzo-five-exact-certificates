Handoff assessment

The portfolio has 49 rows. Exactly 38 cases received new screens, totaling
79.65282005001791 solver seconds. Every one stopped at its trust radius and
none beat its saved comparison ceiling. This is not an exhaustion argument.
Some of those comparisons, including n130 and n154, now trail the SQUISH intake.

The n130 contact closure and n292 corner-resolution mechanisms are already
consumed by the retained endpoints. Final numerical stationarity/rank diagnostics
identify no demonstrated remaining feasible descent. The handoff correctly
declines to nominate another arrangement solely from low contact degree or
near-tied axes. No unrestricted rigidity or optimality follows from those tests.

The most concrete scoped proof opportunity is n105, where an exact dual exists.
However, merely extending it to tiny orientation boxes is routine. A simple
analytic bound already gives a baseline for that experiment:

Let epsilon bound every |t_i-t_i^0|. Keep the selected supporting inequalities,
allow all centers to vary, and keep the 103 dual weights. Let P be the sum of
weights on pair rows and W the sum on wall rows. From the validated base dual
let K0 be its corrected positive objective coefficient and B0 its constant.
Then a valid lower bound is

  L >= -(B0 + (4P+2W)epsilon)/(K0 + 4P epsilon),

provided the numerator constant is negative and the denominator is positive.
The half-angle identity gives
  ||u(t)-u(t0)||^2 = 4(t-t0)^2/((1+t^2)(1+t0^2)) <= 4epsilon^2.
Vertex-offset changes are at most 2epsilon in Euclidean norm. A pair-row
constant therefore changes by at most 4epsilon and a wall-row constant by at
most 2epsilon. Each pair row's center-coefficient l1 change is at most
8epsilon; half that enters the corrected objective coefficient. Nonnegative
weights and containment give the displayed bound. This is a direct analytic
estimate conditional on the validated base dual, not a claim of novelty.

Exact rational evaluation gives U-minus-lower approximately:
  epsilon=2^-40: 4.115100844949538e-10
  epsilon=2^-60: 3.9244659853856133e-16
  epsilon=2^-80: 3.742662415872205e-22.
Thus the proposed tiny-box diagnostic is not enough to justify another long
campaign. A more useful experiment is to preserve cancellations and exploit
center/contact constraints to certify a materially larger neighborhood, or
identify a genuinely feasible improving motion exposed by a failed bound.
The accompanying proof prompt sets that scope and a bounded stopping rule.
